How EarnQube works

Start with your account, follow the instructions and let the task review process confirm qualifying submissions.

01

Use the registration link to open your EarnQube member account. Keep your sign-in details secure.

02

03

ⓘ
Know the task conditions

Not every activity is available at all times. Promotional tasks may have specific campaign periods and proof requirements; quiz and challenge tasks may require correct or qualifying answers. Check your member account for the current rules before you begin.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Important details to understand before joining and participating.

What activities are featured?

WhatsApp Status Promotion, Product Advertising, Daily Trivia, Quick Math Challenge and Colour Recognition Challenge.

How much is the displayed reward?

Each featured activity displays UGX 500 for an approved qualifying completion. Check the individual task for its exact conditions.

Is every activity always available?

No. Availability can depend on current campaigns and platform updates. Sign in to see which activities are currently open.

When is a reward approved?

Submissions are subject to the activity's instructions, eligibility requirements and review. Completing a task does not automatically guarantee approval.

Where do I access the activities?

Create an account or sign in through the member portal, then check the available activities and instructions in your account.

How can I get help?

Contact the EarnQube support team through WhatsApp or email using the details at the bottom of this page.
