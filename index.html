Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Important details to understand before joining and participating.

What activities are featured?

Moderated chat, TikTok videos, YouTube videos, sponsored-content tasks, Dream League Soccer challenges, WhatsApp Status Promotion, Product Advertising, Daily Trivia, Quick Math and Colour Recognition.

How much are the displayed rewards?

Moderated chat: UGX 10,000; TikTok and YouTube video tasks: UGX 5,000 each; sponsored-content tasks: UGX 2,000; original five activities: UGX 2,500 each. DLS challenges: UGX 5,000. Approval conditions apply.

Is every activity always available?

No. Availability can depend on current campaigns and platform updates. Sign in to see which activities are currently open.

Are chat and video activities safe?

Chat activities should be moderated and kept on-platform. Never share your address, phone number, passwords, private photos or other personal details with strangers. Video tasks should not ask you to manipulate likes, views or subscriptions.

When is a reward approved?

Submissions are subject to the activity's instructions, eligibility requirements and review. Completing a task does not automatically guarantee approval.

Where do I access the activities?

Create an account or sign in through the member portal, then check the available activities and instructions in your account.

How can I get help?

Contact the EarnQube support team through WhatsApp or email using the details at the bottom of this page.
